Once upon a time, there was a little girl who was loved by everyone, especially her grandmother. One day, her grandmother gave her a red hood to wear, and from then on she was called Little Red Riding Hood.

One morning, her mother asked her to take food to her sick grandmother who lived deep in the forest. She warned her not to leave the path.

While walking through the forest, Little Red Riding Hood met a wolf. The wolf asked where she was going and suggested she pick flowers for her grandmother. While she was distracted, the wolf ran ahead to the grandmother’s house.

The wolf tricked the grandmother and swallowed her. Then he put on her clothes and lay in her bed.

When Little Red Riding Hood arrived, she noticed something strange about her grandmother.

“What big eyes you have,” she said.
“The better to see you with,” replied the wolf.

“What big teeth you have!”
“The better to eat you with!” cried the wolf, and he swallowed her.

Soon after, a huntsman heard the wolf snoring. He cut open the wolf’s belly and rescued Little Red Riding Hood and her grandmother. They filled the wolf’s belly with stones, and the wolf died.

Little Red Riding Hood learned her lesson and promised never to leave the path again. She lived happily ever after.
